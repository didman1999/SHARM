Ras Mohammed National Park Snorkeling Trip

Ras Mohammed, South Sinai 8 Hours Group Trip Pickup: 8:00 AM Max: 25 guests 4.9 (128 reviews)

Embark on an unforgettable journey to Ras Mohammed National Park, one of the top 10 diving and snorkeling destinations in the world. Located at the southern tip of the Sinai Peninsula where the Gulf of Aqaba meets the Gulf of Suez, this protected marine reserve is home to over 1,000 species of fish and 200 species of coral.

  • 8:00 AM — Hotel pickup by comfortable air-conditioned bus
  • 9:00 AM — Arrive at Ras Mohammed National Park entrance
  • 9:30 AM — First snorkeling stop at the Magic Bay coral reef
  • 10:30 AM — Visit the Mangrove Channel and earthquake crack
  • 11:30 AM — Second snorkeling stop at the famous Yolanda Reef
  • 12:30 PM — Buffet lunch with soft drinks on board / at beach
  • 1:30 PM — Free time for swimming, sunbathing and photos
  • 2:30 PM — Third snorkeling stop (Hidden Bay)
  • 3:30 PM — Begin return journey to hotels
  • 4:00 PM — Arrive back at your hotel
