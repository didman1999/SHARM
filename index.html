FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a trip?

Simply select your desired trip, choose your date and number of guests, and click 'Book Now'. You'll receive a confirmation with your booking number. No online payment required — pay on arrival!

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the trip. For last-minute cancellations, please contact us via WhatsApp and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Do you provide hotel pickup?

Yes! We provide free hotel pickup and drop-off for most trips within the Sharm El Sheikh area. Some locations may have a small additional fee.

Are trips safe for children?

Many of our trips are family-friendly and suitable for children. Look for the 'Family Friendly' badge on trip cards. We provide child life jackets and safety equipment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We currently accept cash payment on arrival (USD, EUR, EGP). No advance payment required. We're working on adding online payment options for your convenience.
